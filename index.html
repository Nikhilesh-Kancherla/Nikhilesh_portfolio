About Me

Who I Am

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering student at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I'm drawn to the intersection of engineering rigor and creative possibility — the space where AI solves real problems and design makes those solutions feel natural.

On the technical side, I work with computer vision systems, deep learning models, and full-stack web applications. My eye-fatigue detection project reflects my approach: combining geometric analysis, neural networks, and real-time processing to build something genuinely useful.

On the creative side, I explore augmented reality with Snapchat Lens Studio, experiment with character design and visual storytelling, and think carefully about how interfaces communicate and feel. I believe great AI products are as much about experience as they are about accuracy.

I'm always experimenting, always learning — and looking for opportunities where both sides of that equation matter.
